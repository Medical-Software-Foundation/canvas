Oura Ring

Checking

Share your Oura Ring summaries with your care team. They will see:

  • Sleep score, total sleep, resting heart rate and HRV
  • Readiness and daily steps
  • Overnight blood oxygen

Your care team sees this data only while you are connected. It is read from Oura when they open your chart and is not saved in your health record. Oura Ring data is wellness data, not a medical measurement.
